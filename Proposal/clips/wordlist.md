## Notes

- Some rows are near-minimal pairs rather than exact ones (Nepali lexicon doesn't always supply a clean minimal pair). Swap in better minimal pairs during recording if a rater/native-speaker check finds a more natural one.
- ष and स are often already merged in casual speech — that's expected and is part of what makes this pair "fuzzy"; record natural pronunciation, don't force an artificial distinction.
- Filenames use `bim` as `speaker_id` for this solo pilot pass. If more speakers join later, follow the same pattern with their own `speaker_id`.
